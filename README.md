# ai-research-log-agent

Turn any research topic into a structured, cited brief — automatically saved to a file and logged to Google Sheets.

Built with Claude Opus (Anthropic) and the Google Sheets API.

---

## What This Does

You give it a topic. It searches the web, writes a structured report, saves it as a file, and logs the results to your Google Sheet — all in one command.

No manual searching. No copy-pasting. No formatting.

---

## Use Cases

- Competitive analysis and market landscape briefs
- Due diligence on acquisition targets, partners, or vendors
- Pre-call sales prep and account research
- Regulatory and policy change monitoring
- Board meeting and executive briefings
- Marketing scripts and messaging in your brand voice
- Industry trend monitoring and technology landscape reviews
